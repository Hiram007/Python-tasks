What implementing this requires

An application registration in the Casidra Entra ID tenant, a client secret held as an environment secret, group claims mapped to roles, conditional access and MFA policies applied by the identity team, and an SCIM or scheduled provisioning job that follows the employee record. None of this is done here, and no tenant, credential or connector exists in this prototype.
