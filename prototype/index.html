Every new starter, the approvals they are waiting on, and the tasks that must land before day one. Coordinators, departments and managers work from this single view.

Design prototype. Every person, date and department below is fictional sample data. Nothing is connected to a backend, and authentication, permissions, approvals and email delivery are not implemented — they are shown here so the design can be reviewed.
