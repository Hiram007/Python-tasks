Workflow & checklist templates

The templates that decide which departmental actions an arrival or departure case must complete, in which order, with which due dates and which approvals. Editing a template changes every future case — it never rewrites a case already in progress.

Activation approvals

Design prototype. No template can be edited, activated or retired here. In production the template library lives in the Django application, activation requires an approved administrative review, and every version change is written to the audit trail.

Status transitions & approvals

Which moves are permitted, and who may make them.

Not started → In progress Any task owner assigned to the step Permitted
In progress → Awaiting approval Task owner submits completion evidence Permitted
Awaiting approval → Complete Designated approver records a decision Approver only
Awaiting approval → Returned Approver returns for correction · comment required Approver only
Any → Complete (override) HR administrator, with a mandatory comment Override
Step deleted after case opened Not permitted — the case keeps its checklist Blocked

Transitions are enforced server-side in production
